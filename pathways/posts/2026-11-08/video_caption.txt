Listen for your "always" and "never."

"You always cancel." "I never get it right." "Nobody listens to me."

Words like that feel true in the moment. But ask: every single time? Not once the other way? Most of the time you can find an exception. That's the story stretching past the facts.

The honest version is usually smaller: "You've cancelled twice this month, and it hurt." It's truer, and it's a lot easier for someone to hear.

Try it: for one week, notice each "always" or "never," in your head or out loud. Pick one and rewrite it as what actually happened.

Then ask: what was the big word protecting me from saying?
