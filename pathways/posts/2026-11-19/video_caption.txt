Stuck in your head? Move first.

An NIH lecture by NYU neuroscientist Wendy Suzuki described studies where exercise went with better attention and mood. She also said the best amount isn't known yet.

Try it: when a decision keeps looping, walk first. Then ask what looks different.

Source: NIH Record (2017). pathways2goodlife.com
