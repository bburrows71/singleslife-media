# Getting to the Root of Misdirected Destruction — Bill Burrows (Patterns)
URL: https://pathways2goodlife.com/root-of-misdirected-destruction.html
Fetched 2026-10-19 (owner present). Sequel to misdirected-destruction.md.

## Intro
- "The blowup is almost never about the dishes."
- "Most relationship damage doesn't come from the actual conflict on the surface." Pain that's unresolved, mislabeled or borrowed.
## Why the target is almost never the real problem
- A feeling too threatening to face gets shifted to a safer target.
- "Your partner is usually the closest, safest, least-likely-to-retaliate option in the room."
- "The actual wound never got touched."
## The tells that you're doing it
- "The reaction is bigger than the trigger." / You can't say what outcome you want. / The same argument keeps coming back. / "Afterward, you feel more ashamed than resolved."
## How to actually de-escalate it
- "Nobody successfully reasons with themselves mid-blowup." Work before and after.
- Catch the gap between trigger and reaction; ask what it's really about; "Separate the feeling from the delivery." ("The feeling is almost always legitimate."); come back to repair:
  "That was bigger than the moment called for — here's what I think it was actually about."
## The takeaway
- "It's a targeting problem." "The dishes were never the problem." "They're just where the real one finally had somewhere to land."
