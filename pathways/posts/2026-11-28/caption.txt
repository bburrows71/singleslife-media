"Fine" isn't a feeling.

"How are you?" "Fine." It comes out so fast we barely hear it. It's polite, it's easy, and it closes the door before anyone has to look inside. Including us.

Sometimes fine is true. Often it's a placeholder for something we haven't stopped to name.

Try this today. Count how many times you say "fine," out loud or in your own head. Each time, pause and ask: what's actually here? Tired. Worried. Hurt. Lonely. Relieved. Pick the closest word, even if you only say it to yourself.

Then, once this week, try the truer word with someone you trust. "Honestly? I'm a little worn down."

Afterward, notice what changed when the feeling had a name. And ask the honest question: what was "fine" letting me skip?
