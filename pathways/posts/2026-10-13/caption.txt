You don't have to be the smartest person in the room.

Many of us walk into a room already preparing what we'll say. But speaking only reveals what we already know. Listening opens the door to what we don't.

When you stop trying to prove yourself, you get curious instead of defensive. You ask better questions. Humility turns a conversation from a contest into an exchange.

And listening doesn't make your voice less valuable. It makes your words more thoughtful.
