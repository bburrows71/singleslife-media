A bad day isn't starting over.

You've been doing the work for a while. Then one day goes sideways: you snap, you skip it, you fall back into the old habit.

The trap is the thought that comes next: "I blew it, so what's the point?" That thought turns one rough day into a reason to quit.

But what you practiced last week didn't vanish today. You don't need a fresh start on Monday. You need the smallest version of the thing, tomorrow.

Try it: next time you slip, swap "Why did I fail?" for "What got in the way today, and what would help next time?"
