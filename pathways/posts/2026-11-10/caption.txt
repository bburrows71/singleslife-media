Wait before you hit send.

A hot message feels urgent. It isn't, usually. The urgency is the feeling, not the situation.

Try this:
1. Write it. Every word. Then leave it in drafts.
2. Wait twenty minutes. Walk, breathe, do something else.
3. Read it out loud, the way they'll hear it. Would you say this to their face, in this tone?

Most of the time you'll cut something. Sometimes you'll cut all of it. Either way, what goes out is something you chose, not something the moment chose for you.

Next time a message is hot, notice what you changed after reading it aloud.
