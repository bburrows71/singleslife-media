Tell one person what you're working on.

Most of us keep our inner work private. The temper we're trying to cool, the habit of going quiet, the "I'm fine" we're trying to stop saying. If nobody knows, nobody sees us slip.

That feels safer. It also makes it easy to let the change quietly fade, without anyone noticing. Sometimes not even us.

Try this. Pick one person. Not your loudest cheerleader, not your harshest critic. Someone who will ask how it's going and actually listen to the answer.

Then say it in one plain sentence: "I'm working on ___." Not a promise. Not a plan. Just letting someone see it.

Afterward, notice what you felt saying it out loud. Relief? Embarrassment? The urge to take it back? Whatever showed up is worth paying attention to.
