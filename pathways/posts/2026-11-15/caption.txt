Write the thing you won't say out loud.

Nobody has to read it. That's the point.

Most of us carry one sentence we haven't said to anyone. Sometimes we haven't even said it to ourselves. "I'm not happy here." "I miss them." "I'm scared I'm the problem." It sits in the back of your head and quietly steers things.

Try this. Take one page that no one else will see. Write the sentence plainly, the way you'd never say it in a room. No softening, no explaining, no "but."

Then read it back once and ask: is this true? What have I been doing so I don't have to look at it?

You don't have to act on it today. Keep the page or tear it up. Just let it be said once, honestly. That's often where the real work starts.
