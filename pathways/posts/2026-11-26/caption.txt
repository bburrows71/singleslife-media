Fixer, peacekeeper, or joker?

Put most of us in a room full of people and we slip into a part. Somebody gets tense and you smooth it over. Somebody has a problem and you're already solving it. Things get heavy and you're the one with the joke.

None of these is bad. Often they're kind. But a role you can't put down isn't really a choice anymore.

Try this. Think about the group you'll be with next, family, friends, coworkers. Which part do you usually play? Then ask: when did I learn this? What do I think would happen if nobody played it?

If you're at a gathering this week, try putting the role down for one evening. Let a tense moment sit. Let someone else fix it. Skip the joke you'd usually make.

Then notice what you feel in the gap where the role used to go. Who are you when you're not playing it?
