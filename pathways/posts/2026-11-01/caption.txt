Do you pull back right when things start going well?

Notice the timing. It often says more than the reason does.

Think of the last time you went quiet, picked a small fight, or suddenly got too busy. What was going well right before it?

Then look at the reason you gave yourself. It may be real. Ask whether it's the whole story.

Last question: what did getting closer feel like, right before you stepped back?

Try it: next time you feel the urge to pull away from something good, name it to yourself and stay a little longer than you want to. Noticing comes first.
