Is willpower really limited?

In Stanford experiments, students who believed willpower runs out did worse on concentration tests after a tiring task. One set of studies with students, not a rule. Next time you think "I'm out of willpower," ask: am I tired, or telling myself I'm done?

Source: Association for Psychological Science (Job, Dweck & Walton, Psychological Science, 2010)
