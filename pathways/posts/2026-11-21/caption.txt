Ten minutes. No input.

Most of us fill every quiet gap before we notice it. The phone in line, a podcast in the car, the TV on in the background. It feels like rest. Sometimes it's just volume.

Try this once this weekend. Ten minutes with no phone, no music, no podcast, no screen. You're not meditating and you're not trying to empty your head. You're just not adding anything to it.

Then notice what comes up. A worry you've been stepping around. Someone you've been meaning to call. A conversation you keep putting off. Don't solve it. Just let it be there.

Afterward, write down the first thing that surfaced and ask yourself one question: what have I been using the noise to avoid hearing?
