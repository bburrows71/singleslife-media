Listening isn't waiting for your turn to talk.

Real listening asks you to set aside the reply already forming in your mind and give your full attention to what's being said.

It also asks you to notice what isn't said: hesitation, silence, context, the meaning beneath the words.

That kind of quiet isn't weakness. It's discipline. It takes confidence to admit someone else may know something you don't.
