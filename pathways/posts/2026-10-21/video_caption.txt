Brooding or reflecting?

Both mean turning something over in your head. One study separated them:
Brooding: comparing where you are with where you think you should be, and staying there.
Reflecting: turning inward on purpose to work the problem.

In that study, brooding went with more depression symptoms, at the time and later. Reflecting went with fewer over time.

Try it: next time a thought loops, ask "Am I comparing, or am I solving?" If it's comparing, ask one question: what's one thing I could try?

Coaching, not therapy. If low mood sticks around, talk to a professional.

Source: Treynor, Gonzalez & Nolen-Hoeksema, "Rumination Reconsidered," Cognitive Therapy and Research (2003)
