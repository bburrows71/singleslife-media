What happened, or the story you told yourself about it?

Try this with one moment from this week that you keep replaying.

Draw a line down a page. On the left, write only the facts: what a camera would have caught. Who said what. What happened next.

On the right, write the story: what you decided it meant about them, about you, about where this is going.

Then read them side by side. Where did the story go further than the facts? The facts are fixed. The story is yours to check.
