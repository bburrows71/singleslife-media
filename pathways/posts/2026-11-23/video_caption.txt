Missing people is a real signal.

In an MIT study, after 10 hours alone, photos of people together activated a midbrain craving area much like food photos did after fasting. One lab study, mostly students.

Try it: when you feel the pull toward someone, send them one real message.

Source: MIT News / Nature Neuroscience (2020). pathways2goodlife.com
