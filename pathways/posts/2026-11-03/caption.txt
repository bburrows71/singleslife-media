What do you complain about most?

It usually points to what you care about most.

Try this. Think back over the last two weeks and write down the three things you grumbled about more than once. The traffic, the meeting that ran long, the friend who always cancels. Whatever came up.

Under each one, ask: what would I have to care about to be this bothered? Time. Respect. Being counted on. Write the one word.

Then look at the gap. If you care about it that much, are you acting like it? Or only noticing when it's missing?

Pick one word from your list and do one thing this week that protects it.
