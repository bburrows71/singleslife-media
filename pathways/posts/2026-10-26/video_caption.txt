Tired makes feelings louder.

In a UC Berkeley study, healthy young adults stayed awake for about 35 hours, then looked at upsetting images in a brain scanner. Their brains' emotional centers reacted over 60 percent more than those of people who had slept. The lead researcher said the results point to sleep doing something for how we handle emotions.

That doesn't make every reaction a sleep problem. It does make one question worth asking before a sharp reply late at night: am I upset, or am I tired? If it's both, the conversation can wait until morning.

Try it: for one week, jot down your sharpest reactions and how you slept the night before. See what lines up.

Coaching, not medical advice.

Source: UC Berkeley News; Yoo, Walker et al., Current Biology (2007)
