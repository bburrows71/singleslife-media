Nobody else is going to remind you, so here's your October home check. 🔧

Six jobs, one afternoon:
1. Swap the furnace filter (write the size on the unit)
2. Test every smoke and CO alarm
3. Find your main water shut-off and make sure it turns
4. Clean the dryer lint trap and vent
5. Check door weatherstripping for drafts
6. Stock a flashlight and fresh batteries

Save this for the weekend, and send it to a friend who runs a home on their own.
