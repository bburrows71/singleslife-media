# The Loop That Keeps You Stuck — Bill Burrows (Patterns · Getting unstuck)
URL: https://pathways2goodlife.com/the-loop-that-keeps-you-stuck.html
Fetched 2026-10-19 (owner present). About rumination; keep it coaching, not mental-health advice; the article itself points to professionals for loops that are frequent or hard to interrupt.

## Intro
- "You replay it one more time, sure this pass will finally make sense of it."
- "Reflection asks a question and goes looking for an answer." Rumination asks the same question ("Why did this happen to me?") without moving.
- "Most of the time you're just wearing a groove into the same thought."
## What it actually costs you
- "The loop doesn't just feel bad." Costs: mental exhaustion, lower self-esteem, sleep trouble from replaying in bed, trouble focusing on the present, inaction because thinking replaces doing.
- "Breaking the loop isn't the same as solving everything at once."
## Four ways to break it ("The goal isn't forcing yourself to stop thinking.")
  1. Recognize it: name it, "I'm ruminating right now."
  2. Challenge it: fact or interpretation? Is it helping? What would you tell a friend?
  3. Shift to action: one small useful step (a walk, a task, reaching out to someone you trust).
  4. Redirect attention on purpose. "That's not avoidance — that's the skill."
## The takeaway
- "You're not required to finish a thought just because it started."
- "The hundredth replay never makes more sense than the first one did."
