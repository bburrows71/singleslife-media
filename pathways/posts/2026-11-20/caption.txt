Quiet with some. Loud with others.

Most of us don't show up the same way with everyone. Around some people we shrink: shorter answers, swallowed opinions, "whatever you want." Around others we get bigger: arguing, joking harder, talking over.

Try this. Make two lists. People you go quiet around. People you get loud around.

Then look for the thread. What do the quiet names have in common? The loud ones? Maybe it's who you want approval from, or who you expect to push back.

Last question: what is each one protecting? Quiet might keep the peace. Loud might keep you from feeling small. Neither is wrong. Both are worth seeing.

This week, pick one name. Next time you're with them, notice the moment your volume changes, and what you were feeling right before it.
