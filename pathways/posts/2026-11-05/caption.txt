Your brain is always guessing.

Researchers at the Max Planck Institute for Psycholinguistics and Radboud University had people listen to stories while their brain activity was recorded. They estimated how predictable each word was in context.

What they found: unexpected words drew stronger brain responses. The brain was predicting at many levels at once, from meaning and grammar down to speech sounds. And it wasn't only in special moments. In the lead researcher's words, "the predictive machinery is always turned on."

Here's the coaching part. If your brain is guessing what comes next in a story, it's worth asking what it's guessing in your conversations, especially the familiar ones.

Try it: today, notice one moment you finish someone's sentence in your head. Then let them finish it. Were you right? What did you almost miss?

Source: Max Planck Institute for Psycholinguistics, "Our brain is a prediction machine that is always active" (Heilbron et al., PNAS, 2022)
