Stop one thing first.

New starts feel good. Five new habits on the first of the month, a fresh plan, a clean slate. Then the old stuff is still there, quietly undoing the new.

Most of us already know the one habit that keeps cancelling the rest. Saying "I'm fine" when we're not. Replaying the argument at night. Putting off the conversation that matters.

Try it: pick one thing to stop this month. Make it small enough that you'd notice when you do it. Finish the sentence: "This month I'm stopping ___."

Then the more useful question: what was that habit doing for you?
