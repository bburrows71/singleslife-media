A boundary or a punishment?

From the outside they can look the same. The difference is who they're about.

A boundary says what you will do: "If the yelling starts, I'll leave the room." It holds whether they change or not.

A punishment is built to make them feel it: the silent treatment, the withheld yes.

One quiet question: would this still work if they never noticed?
