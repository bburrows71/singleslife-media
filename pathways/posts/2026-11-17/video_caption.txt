What did you want them to understand?

After a rough conversation, most of us replay it. Who said what. Who was wrong. What we should have said back. It can go on for days.

Try a different question instead. Finish this sentence: "What I wanted them to understand was ___." Keep it to one thing. Not the whole case, just the heart of it.

Then look back honestly. Did you actually say that, plainly? Or did it get lost somewhere in being right, getting louder, or going quiet?

If it never got said, you don't need a rematch. You need one clear sentence, said when you're both calm: "What I was trying to say is ___."
