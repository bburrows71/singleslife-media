Your brain is always guessing.

In a study from the Max Planck Institute and Radboud University, people listened to stories while their brain activity was recorded. Unexpected words drew stronger responses, and the predicting never switched off.

Try it: notice one moment today you finish someone's sentence in your head. Then let them finish. Were you right?

Source: Max Planck Institute for Psycholinguistics (Heilbron et al., PNAS, 2022)
