Start smaller than feels useful.

A plan that only works on your best day will stall on your ordinary ones. And most days are ordinary.

So shrink it until it's almost silly. Not "have the hard talk." Write the first sentence you'd say. Not "journal every day." One line.

Then test it on a bad day. If you can do it tired, busy and annoyed, it's the right size. You can always do more.
