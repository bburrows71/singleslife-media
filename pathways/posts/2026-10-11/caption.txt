First listen. Then understand. Then speak.

Animals survive not by making the first sound, but by listening first. Only then do they call out.

Real listening is more than waiting for your turn to talk. It means setting aside the reply forming in your mind, and noticing what isn't said: hesitation, silence, the meaning beneath the words.

That kind of quiet isn't weakness. It's discipline.
