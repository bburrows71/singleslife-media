Who are you still trying to be?

Some of the pressure you carry doesn't come from today. It comes from an older version of you that you're still trying to live up to.

Try this. Finish the sentence: "I'm supposed to be the one who..." Write whatever comes. The one who never needs help. The one who always holds it together. The one who has it all figured out by now.

Then ask: when did that version make sense? Who were you then, and what was it protecting you from?

Last question: does living up to it help the life you have now? Or does it mostly leave you feeling behind?

You don't have to throw all of it out. Keep the part that still serves you. Then write one sentence for the rest: "I don't have to be ___ anymore."
