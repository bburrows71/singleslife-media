Your fall home check, before the cold sets in.

From our complete checklist for living alone:
• Flush the water heater (fall is the time)
• Clean the dryer vent and clear the gutters
• Look under sinks and around the toilet for drips
• Swap the HVAC filter every 1 to 3 months

Small drips are cheap to fix. Water damage is not.

Don't try to remember all of it. Put each task on a weekly, monthly or seasonal reminder and let the calendar carry it.
