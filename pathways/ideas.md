## Self-honesty
1. [used 2026-10-20] Three questions for the end of a hard day: What did I feel today that I didn't say? Where did I run on autopilot? What's one thing I'd do differently tomorrow?
2. [used 2026-11-28] "Fine" isn't a feeling. Try naming what's actually there, even just to yourself.
3. [used 2026-10-27] What happened vs. the story you told yourself about it: write them in two columns.
4. [used 2026-11-08] Listen for your "always" and "never." They're usually the story talking.
5. [used 2026-11-03] What you complain about most points to what you care about most.
6. [used 2026-11-15] Write down the thing you won't say out loud. Nobody has to read it.

## Relationship patterns
7. [used 2026-10-28] Taking space without disappearing: "I need an hour. I'll call you at 8."
8. [used 2026-10-22] A boundary protects you. A punishment is meant to hurt them. Know which one you're setting.
9. [used 2026-11-10] Before you hit send: wait 20 minutes, then read it out loud.
10. [used 2026-11-17] After a rough conversation, ask: what did I want them to understand?
11. [used 2026-11-04] An apology with "but" in it is an argument.
12. [used 2026-11-12] Ask for what you need before it turns into resentment.
13. [used 2026-11-01] Do you pull back right when things start going well? Notice the timing.
