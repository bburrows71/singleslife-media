"No" doesn't need a speech.

Listen to what comes out after you turn something down. "I can't, because work's been crazy, and my sister's in town, and I'm so sorry, I feel terrible..."

That long version usually isn't for them. It's for us: to head off their disappointment, or to earn a permission we never needed.

You can be kind and short at the same time. "I can't make it, but thanks for asking." Then stop.

Try it: next time you say no this week, use one sentence. Then notice what you feel in the quiet after it. Relief? Guilt? The pull to add one more reason? That pull is worth getting to know.
