Did you say yes, or did you feel guilty?

Try a short audit this weekend.

List every commitment you're carrying right now: the favors, the plans, the "sure, I can do that."

Next to each one, ask: did I want this, or did I say yes so I wouldn't feel bad saying no?

Notice which ones feel heavier. Guilt yeses tend to be the ones you keep putting off and then resent doing.

Pick one to rethink. Not all of them, just one. Then pay attention to the next time a yes starts to come out before you've decided.
