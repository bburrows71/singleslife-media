What are you still carrying?

Try this. Ten minutes, one page.

Write down every unfinished thing you're holding in your head: the call you owe, the decision you keep putting off, the conversation you haven't had. Don't sort it. Don't judge it.

Then pick one. Not the biggest, just the one you could close this week. Write the first step next to it.

Last part: notice how it feels to see it on paper instead of circling in your head.
