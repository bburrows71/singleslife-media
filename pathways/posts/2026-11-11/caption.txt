Measure against last month's you.

When you feel behind, it's worth asking: behind whom? Usually it's someone else's highlight reel. You see their best moments and none of their hard days. That was never a fair test.

Try a different one. Look back four weeks. What did you handle a little better this month? A pause before a sharp reply. Saying the honest thing instead of "I'm fine." Walking away from an argument you used to finish.

Change rarely looks like a before-and-after photo. It looks like the same moment, handled a bit differently.

Try it: finish this sentence. "A month ago I would have ___. Now I ___." Write it somewhere you'll find it. Read it again next month.
