The compliment you wave off.

"Oh, it was nothing." "Anyone could have done it." "You're just being nice."

Most of us have one compliment we can't let land. It comes out so fast we barely hear the kind thing before we've pushed it away.

Try this. Think back over the last month. Find one compliment you brushed off and write down the exact words someone said to you.

Then ask yourself honestly: what would it mean if it were true? What would you have to stop believing about yourself?

That's often where the brush-off comes from. Not modesty, but an old picture of yourself that the compliment doesn't fit.

This week: the next time someone says something kind, answer with "thank you." Then stop. Notice what you feel in the pause where the brush-off used to go.
