Ask before it turns bitter.

Resentment rarely shows up out of nowhere. Often it's a need that sat unspoken long enough to sour.

Try this:
1. Notice the small sting. The sigh when they're late again. The quiet scorekeeping. That's usually a need talking.
2. Hints aren't asks. Hoping they'll notice isn't the same as saying it. People can't meet a need they don't know about.
3. Say it small and early. "Could you text if you're running late? It helps me." One need, one sentence.

They might say no. That's still better than a year of keeping score.

This week: find one thing you keep quietly resenting. Ask yourself what you need there, then say it in one plain sentence.
