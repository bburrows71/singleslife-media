Talking without listening is a loop.

Our minds form loops of behavior, and once they're set, we return to them again and again. If we've trained ourselves to speak without listening, that pattern shapes how we act and react.

Breaking the loop starts with a simple choice: practice silence for once and give someone else your full attention.

In that pause, a new habit can begin.
