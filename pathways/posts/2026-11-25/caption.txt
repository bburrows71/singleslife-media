Decided. Then reopened.

Most of us have one decision we keep making over and over. The job, the move, the friendship we stepped back from. We land on an answer, feel settled for a day or two, and then the whole thing is back on the table.

Notice what usually brings it back. Is it new information? Or is it the same doubt, wearing a different outfit?

Try this. Take one page and write the decision down once: what you chose, why you chose it, and what you knew at the time. Plain words. Then set a simple rule for yourself: you reopen it only if something in the facts actually changes. A bad mood isn't a fact. Neither is 2 a.m.

Then ask the more honest question: what does keeping this open protect you from? Sometimes it's the grief of what you gave up. Sometimes it's the fear of being wrong. Both are worth feeling directly instead of re-deciding around them.
