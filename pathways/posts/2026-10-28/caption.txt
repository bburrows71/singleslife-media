Take space. Don't disappear.

Needing to step away in a hard moment is fair. How you step away is what the other person feels.

Space has an end: "I need an hour. I'll call you at 8."
Disappearing doesn't: the phone goes quiet, and they're left guessing. Guessing usually fills in the worst.

Then the part that matters most: come back at 8. That's what makes it safe to ask for space next time.

Try it: write your sentence now, before you need it. What you need, how long, when you'll be back.
